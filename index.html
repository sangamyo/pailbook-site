Every day’s milk on a calendar. The bill ready when the milkman asks.

For homes that buy milk from a milkman (doodhwala) or a dairy. Your usual quantity is filled in every day; tap a day only when it changes. At bill time, Pailbook shows the litres, the amount, what you paid and what is still due.

Get it on Google Play

Android, coming soon to Google Play. No ads, no in-app purchases, no account and no sign-up.

  • A calendar that fills itself

    Set the usual quantity for each milk type, morning and evening. Tap any day to skip it or change the litres.

  • Bills that match your milkman’s

    Weekly, monthly on his own cycle (for example the 10th to the 9th) or any dates. Price changes, extras like curd or paneer, previous due and payments are all counted.

  • Share in one tap

    Send the bill as a PDF or as a WhatsApp or plain-text message. You choose the app and press send yourself.

Pailbook only keeps records. It never collects, holds or transfers money. You pay your milkman the way you always do, and note the payment in the app.

Your data stays on your phone

There is no account and no Pailbook server. Milkmen, daily quantities, prices, bills and payments are saved on your phone, and in your own Google account backup if your phone’s backup is on. Use Backup & restore in Settings to save a backup file wherever you like (for example Google Drive) and to move to a new phone.
